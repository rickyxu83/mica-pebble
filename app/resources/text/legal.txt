# Legal
## AI providers
Mica sends prompts and relevant context from the Pebble phone app to the AI provider or custom endpoint you configure. Mica does not provide a hosted AI helper service. Provider terms and privacy policies apply.
Optional Android enhanced mode may route processing through the separately installed Android companion.
